Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

UGC Command Center is a content management tool used to organize creator campaigns, social media accounts, content, tasks, and performance information.

Information We Collect

UGC Command Center may store information you provide directly, including campaign information, content records, account information, tasks, and performance data.

Connected Social Media Accounts

If you connect a supported social media account, the service may access information authorized by you through that platform's official API. This may include account information, published content, and content performance metrics, depending on the permissions you grant.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the application's content management, campaign tracking, analytics, and automation features.

Third-Party Services

UGC Command Center may use third-party infrastructure and APIs to provide its functionality. Information processed by those providers is subject to their applicable privacy and security practices.

Data Control

Users may disconnect connected accounts and may request deletion of information associated with the service.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the operator of UGC Command Center.
